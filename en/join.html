Join Us

We are looking for people to work with.

The Laboratory of Microbial Symbiosis studies the relationships microorganisms form with their hosts and how those relationships evolve, using lichens, plants, arthropods and aquatic systems. Field collection, culture, sequencing, metabolomics and bioinformatics all happen within one lab, and a single student can follow a project through all of them.

Open positions

  • Graduate students (M.S., Ph.D., integrated M.S./Ph.D.) — open year-round
  • Undergraduate research students — open year-round
  • Postdoctoral researchers — funding is limited at present, and a position normally needs to be matched with a suitable external fellowship. We are glad to discuss options and to apply together.

Who we are looking for

  • People curious about the diversity of organisms and how they evolve
  • People willing to work both in the field and at the bench
  • People open to learning data analysis and programming (No prior skill is required. It can be learned here.)

What you will learn

  • Collection, identification and culture of lichens and fungi
  • Genome and metabarcoding sequencing, and analysis of the resulting data
  • Metabolomic and chemical analysis of secondary metabolites
  • Writing papers and presenting at conferences

How to apply

Send an email to syoh@changwon.ac.kr with the following. A brief, informal message is fine.

  1. A short introduction and the research topics that interest you
  2. A CV or academic transcript
  3. When you would like to start

International applicants

Enquiries from outside Korea are welcome. Research in the lab can be carried out entirely in English, and lab meetings and supervision are no obstacle.

For life outside the lab, we do recommend picking up basic Korean. It is not a requirement for the research, but it makes a real difference to how comfortable your time in Changwon is.

The Global Korea Scholarship (GKS), run by the Korean government, supports international students in degree programmes here, and we are happy to advise on the application. For postdoctoral positions, funding is limited at present and is usually arranged by matching a suitable external fellowship; please write to us and we can look at the options together.
