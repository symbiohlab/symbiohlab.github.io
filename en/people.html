I study the ecology and evolution of symbiotic microorganisms. By comparing symbiotic systems as different as lichens, plants, arthropods and aquatic environments, I ask how associations between organisms work, how they change with environment, and how they came about. Describing and naming what we find is part of that work rather than separate from it.
